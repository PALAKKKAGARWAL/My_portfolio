01  /  About

I like problems where the model is only half the work.

Most of what I build sits between a model and the person who has to use it: data ingestion and preprocessing on one side, a working interface on the other. That is how SmartAudit, my student-performance predictor and Local Link all came together — pipelines with a product wrapped around them.

Right now I am deepening machine learning, deep learning and data science alongside my CSE coursework at VIT, working on AI agents after the Google × Kaggle intensive, and contributing production-ready fixes to open source through GSSoC ’26.

Off the keyboard, I run hands-on AI, ML and web workshops as a technical member of IEEE Women in Engineering — teaching a thing is how I find out whether I actually understand it.
